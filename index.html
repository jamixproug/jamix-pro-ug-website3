The sound

A set is a plan, not a playlist.

How a set is built

Afrobeats, amapiano, dancehall, hip-hop, R&B and the classics — selected for the room in front of us, not the algorithm. The shape of the night is mapped before the first track drops.

  1. 01

    Read the room

    Age, energy, seating and programme. The first decisions are practical, not musical.

  2. 02

    Map the energy

    Open warm, lift as the floor fills, hold the peak, then bring it down gently.

  3. 03

    Select wide, then narrow

    Genres move; the thread between them does not. Every transition should feel inevitable.
