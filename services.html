Pricing

No price list. A clear quote.

Fixed rates would be guesswork. A DJ set for a small private dinner and a full venue night are different jobs, with different durations, equipment and travel. So every booking is costed for what it actually needs.

You will receive a written quote that states the date, the scope, what is included and what is not. If the brief changes, the quote changes — before the event, not after.

Any entry or table price shown on an event flyer is set by that event's organiser, not by JAMIX PRO UG. JAMIX PRO UG service pricing is confirmed only in your written quote.

Questions

Before you ask.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can. Popular dates go first. Short-notice requests are still worth asking about — availability is confirmed per date.

Do you travel outside Kampala?

Ask. Travel and accommodation, where required, are scoped and quoted per booking. Nothing outside Kampala is assumed.

Is sound equipment included?

It depends on the service and the venue. Equipment scope is stated in your written quote so there is no ambiguity on the day.

Can we send a must-play list?

Yes — share must-plays and do-not-plays in the brief. The set still responds to the room on the night, which is the point of a live DJ.

How is a date confirmed?

Through the written quote. The confirmation terms, payment details and cancellation or rescheduling arrangements are set out there, and the date is held only once those terms are met.

Do you host private karaoke events?

Yes, karaoke is available as a hosted service for private events and venues. Send the room size, audience and date so the scope can be confirmed.
